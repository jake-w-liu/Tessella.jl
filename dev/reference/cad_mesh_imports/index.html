Independent solid mesh imports

import_step(path) and import_iges(path) import the supported recognized solid layouts into meshes. Existing single-solid imports return Mesh. Multiple explicit independent source roots return a classified MixedMesh. Use fill=false to obtain their closed triangle boundaries without filling.

Each imported solid retains one discrete boundary Surface and Region, separate node/element UIDs, and source identity in physical names. STEP entity IDs or IGES directory-entry IDs determine solid ordering. Filled boundary triangles come from actual tetrahedron incidence; boundary/interior node owners follow that incidence. IGES primitive and solid root directory-entry transforms are applied — any finite nonsingular affine (scale, shear, mirror) on the triangulated product; entity-level transforms inside a root stay rigid for the analytic classifiers.

Strictly separated bounding boxes certify independent solids directly. When boxes overlap an exact geometric certificate decides: facet pairs sharing exactly the same vertex positions with opposed outward windings are conforming contact and weld — each coincident pair becomes one shared Surface entity bound by both Regions, emitted once, with rim nodes deduplicated by exact position. Vertex/edge-only coincident contacts are conforming too but weld nothing: each solid keeps its own coincident boundary node, matching OCC import semantics. A solid strictly enclosed by another (every free boundary vertex inside the other shell, no boundary contact) is a nested Region: with fill=false both surfaces import verbatim, and with fill=true one joint conforming fill — surfaces ordered innermost first — tiles each Region exactly while the enclosed boundary welds into a shared Surface bound by both Regions. Direct containment only welds an interface; a transitively enclosed solid has no interface with the outermost container. Boundaries crossing transversally fragment: exact rational intersection segments between straddling facet pairs cut both shells (facet retriangulation with the 2-D Delaunay kernel, segment constraints and mirrored rim endpoints), each cut piece classifies against the other solids by interior containment probes, and pieces regroup by containment signature into Regions — A only, B only, A∩B — that weld at their coincident cut interfaces under either fill mode. Coplanar non-conforming contacts imprint: facet pairs sharing one rational support plane whose overlaps are partial faces, crossed or collinear rim edges, or vertex/edge-on-face T-junctions (including mismatched shared-quad diagonals) retessellate over one exact planar arrangement per support plane — every on-plane edge sub-segment and edge-plane pierce point enters the 2-D constrained Delaunay triangulation, each cell classifies by exact centroid coverage, overlap regions emit as oppositely wound coincident pieces that weld like conforming contact, and arrangement rim vertices propagate to off-plane incident facets so the rebuilt shells stay watertight. Isolated non-coplanar contacts imprint too: a vertex on a foreign face or edge becomes a Steiner vertex and a blade edge resting on a face becomes a constrained chord sub-edge in every touched facet lacking it, so the contact turns conforming and stays unwelded — each solid keeps its own coincident node. Filled solids weld only when both independent fills emit the identical interface triangulation. Mixed arrangements — crossings composed with welds, coplanar overlays, imprints or enclosures on any pair, aligned positive-area overlaps that merge material, wholly coincident embedded subshells, enclosed solids sharing boundary contact, genuinely interpenetrating volumes, and region boundaries self-touching at isolated vertices — decompose through the same fragment classifier: every cut piece classifies against every shell by containment signature, coincident pieces weld on opposed normals or merge inward on aligned normals, and region shells recurse through the ordinary pipeline. A facet shared by three solids, a fill crossing the interface rim, a cut piece straddling another surface, contacts without an isolated feature description (same-region duplication, non-collinear candidate spreads) and duplicated solids all reject. Genuinely disjoint solids are admitted. Boxes are never used to discover, split or replace source roots. Point-cloud box imports retain their original single-solid behavior. Multi-root IGES identities must be positive, odd, unique and consistent with the directory-entry type. Unsupported geometric entity types are rejected; supported type-406 property records can accompany the geometry. A single-face MANIFOLD_SOLID_BREP on an untrimmed TOROIDAL_SURFACE imports as a full torus; a single-face shell on a SPHERICAL_SURFACE whose boundary loops collapse onto the two placement poles imports as a full sphere (boundary vertices off either pole mean a genuine trim and reject). A three-face shell of one CYLINDRICAL_SURFACE lateral face plus two PLANE caps imports as a full cylinder when the edge-curve inventory certifies it: lateral bounds hold only closed rim CIRCLEs centred on the axis with matching radius and axial normal plus optional axial seam segments, and each cap bounds closed circles sharing one rim inside its plane; partial arcs, off-axis or wrong-radius circles, unclosed rims and non-axial seams are genuine trims and reject. A CONICAL_SURFACE lateral face plus PLANE caps imports as a full cone or frustum when the same inventory certifies it: rim CIRCLEs centred on the axis with axial normals and the radius the semi-angle predicts at that axial coordinate, a closed degenerate edge at the apex for a pointed cone, and seam segments whose carrier line passes through the apex; off-law radii, non-generator seams, missing caps and unclosed rim/apex sets reject as malformed or trimmed. A TOROIDAL_SURFACE lateral face plus two PLANE caps imports as a partial-angle torus segment when the inventory certifies it: rim CIRCLEs of the minor radius centred on the tube circle inside meridian planes containing the axis, optional open seam arcs on surface parallels joining the rims, and caps each binding closed circles of one rim in its meridian plane. The cap face orientations choose between the two complementary sweeps; inconsistent orientations, off-surface parallels and non-meridian rims reject. Use fill=false to import toroidal boundary meshes. Volume filling can fail during boundary recovery. Every STEP solid or primitive wrapped by a unit-bearing shape representation converts by its declared SI or conversion-based length factor into millimetres; a solid shared by incompatible factors rejects as ambiguous and representations without unit declarations keep raw coordinates.

This operation returns a mesh. It does not populate the Model/OCC registry, reconstruct analytic CAD Face/Curve parameter maps or provide general assembly/Boolean topology. A mapped STEP occurrence producer supports one explicit representation root, rigid axis-placement targets, unit-scale Cartesian targets and nested maps of supported source solids. A separate direct product-usage producer admits complete single-root NAUO/CDSR/IDT assembly paths with actual rigid frames and SI source/placement length conversion into millimetre output coordinates. See the direct product-usage interface for nested assemblies. Unsupported assembly transformations reject. Trimmed and periodic NURBS topology is not covered by this mesh-import interface.

Supported planar STEP/IGES Faces can have one outer wire and disjoint inner wires. STEP uses FACEOUTERBOUND when present; otherwise one wire must uniquely contain the others. IGES144 and MSBO510 retain their literal outer/inner wire roles; IGES143 preserves multiple declared boundary groups and certifies one unique containing outer wire. Tessella CDT triangulates the domain without adding unbound vertices. Crossing, touching, overlap, wrong winding, nested holes, ambiguous outer roles and nonplanarity reject. The existing closed-shell and positive-fill checks certify the result; single-wire Faces retain their previous path.

IGES141 admits representation types 0/1. IGES142 uses its 3-D model curve pointer, rather than interpreting its UV pointer as XYZ. UV-only bounded line/composite wires on explicit PlaneSurface190 Form1 use the declared parameter-to-model frame, including finite proper rigid124 Form0 surface transform chains; see IGES plane charts. Implicit108, nonlinear UV and general curved Face trimming are unsupported here. See the Boundary schema and CurveOnSurface schema.
