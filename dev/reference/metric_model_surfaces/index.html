Tensor Surface generation

The explicit tensor overload builds a planar mesh from the model's actual Point and Curve products:

field = MathEvalAnisoField(m11="4+12*x", m22="1", m33="1")
mesh = mesh_model_surface(model, surface_tag, field;
    max_metric_edge=2.01, max_nodes=100, max_triangles=200)

The supplied tensor, including an explicit wrapper, is consumed directly. Global scalar size caps and factors are not applied again. Ordinary mesh_model_surface(model, tag; size_field=field) and API.mesh.generate(2) also consume tensors when algorithm 7 is selected; those paths apply the session's directional size bounds, factor and anisotropy cap.

The native producer uses metric-Delaunay Steiner insertion, conforming edge bisection, flips and vertex removal. It retains full world-coordinate tensor cross terms, equivalent to the tangential pullback B' * M * B. Every edge of the refined triangular product satisfies metric_edge_length(field, a, b) <= max_metric_edge, evaluated at its endpoints and midpoint. This is a sampled contract; it does not bound arbitrary unsampled points of a variable field or promise a minimum angle.

Actual lower vertices retain their UIDs, coordinates, classifications and parameters. Planar faces support holes, geometric curved boundaries, embedded Points and Curves, and recombination algorithms 0–3. An embedded Curve retains literal edges with two incident Surface cells. A supplied Point on a Curve splits that actual chain before full-quad coarsening. Retained lower cells must satisfy the requested metric bound; the producer rejects under-resolution instead of silently replacing owned cells. The triangular edge bound above is checked before recombination and is not a claim about every final quad edge.

The ordinary algorithm-7 dispatcher also has ruled and OCC parametric chart producers. The explicit tensor overload remains planar. Transfinite and extruded Surface construction uses its own producer. Algorithm-7 ordering is native Julia and does not claim identical BAMG chronology or matching tie order.

Periodic Surface masters publish the final refined/recombined constructor to the ordinary owned copy producer. Per-Surface elliptic smoothing is retained as a transfinite attribute, as in the Gmsh manual. It does not reject ordinary algorithm-7 generation.

New vertices and cells carry constructor births. Resource limits, invalid metrics and representability failures return no partial mesh or model mutation; the explicit overload works on a detached model copy. See the session endpoint for publication and additional session restrictions.
